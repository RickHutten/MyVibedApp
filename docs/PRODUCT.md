# Product

## Working concept

A personal ambient dashboard for a tablet or screen in the home. It continuously combines daily information and highlights what is most useful to the user at the current moment.

The product should reduce the need to check several separate apps and remember routine details manually.

## Initial user

The initial product is for one person using a private dashboard at home. Multi-user and household collaboration are not initial assumptions.

## Example outcome

> Leave in 15 minutes. Traffic is heavier than usual. Rain is expected this afternoon. You have two tasks due today.

## Candidate information

- Current weather and forecast
- To-do tasks
- Calendar and schedule
- Recommended departure time based on live travel conditions
- Reminders and information the user asked the app to remember
- Context-aware suggestions and alerts

These are candidate capabilities, not a committed MVP scope.

## Target experience

- Useful at a glance without requiring interaction
- Continuously updated
- Prioritizes timely information instead of showing every available item
- Designed primarily for an always-on tablet display
- Also usable through a normal web browser
- Calm and readable rather than notification-heavy

## Product principles

- Relevance over information density
- Clear next actions over raw data
- User control over integrations, reminders, and personal information
- Graceful behavior when an external service is unavailable
- Privacy appropriate for personal schedules, locations, and reminders

## Open questions

- Which moment should the first version optimize for: morning planning, leaving home, arriving home, or the full day?
- What is the smallest useful MVP?
- Which calendar, task, weather, and travel providers should be integrated first?
- How should users enter and retrieve things they want the app to remember?
- Should processing remain local where possible, or is a hosted service acceptable?
- How much interaction should happen on the dashboard itself?
